Chao Huang

Assistant Professor · The University of Hong Kong

Chao Huang is an Assistant Professor and Ph.D. supervisor at the University of Hong Kong, where he directs the Data Intelligence Lab. His research spans large language models, AI agents, graph machine learning, and recommender systems. His team develops open-source systems including LightRAG, RAG-Anything, DeepCode, AutoAgent, and AI-Researcher, connecting retrieval and knowledge management with autonomous software development and scientific research. He received the Bright Star award at WAIC 2024 and the 2024 Frontier Science Award in Theoretical Computer Science and Information Science (ICBS). Before joining HKU, he was a research scientist at JD Research America and earned his Ph.D. from the University of Notre Dame.

Qilin Qi

Senior Applied Science Manager, Prime Video · Amazon

Qilin Qi leads the Prime Video recommendation science team at Amazon, driving large-scale recommendation, ranking, and whole-page optimization systems for personalized discovery. Previously, he led DoorDash's core consumer machine learning teams, building homepage recommendations, search ranking, and growth ML systems. He has held leadership and research roles across Amazon, Microsoft, Meta, and Apple, with expertise in recommender systems, information retrieval, and applied machine learning at scale.
